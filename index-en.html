Everyday life

Actually quite simple. Once you know how.

Many things in everyday life seem obvious. Until language, uncertainty or a lack of digital experience turns them into a hurdle.

  1. 01

    “What do they actually want from me?”

    Take a photo of a letter or document. digibeg explains clearly what it is about.

  2. 02

    “Can you translate this for me?”

    Speak, type or take a photo. digibeg helps with translating and understanding.

  3. 03

    “Remind me on Tuesday.”

    Organize appointments and reminders simply by voice or text.

  4. 04

    “What do I need to do now?”

    Ask questions and understand the next step better.

How it works

From question to next step.

An example: a letter from the health insurer.

  1. Step 1

    Photograph or upload a document.

    A photo with the camera is enough. Or a file that is already on the device.

  2. Step 2

    digibeg explains what it is about and what matters.

    Summarized in plain language: what it says, what has to be paid, by when, and what the next steps are. Everything can be read aloud on request.

  3. Step 3

    Ask, listen, translate, follow through.

    Afterwards, content can be read aloud or translated. Detected appointments and deadlines can be checked and added to the calendar.

Relatives & companions

Reassurance without surveillance.

Relatives don’t want to monitor. They want to know that important things are taken care of.

digibeg can involve relatives and companions where support is wanted. No more, no less.

  • Everyone chooses at the start: For myself or I accompany someone.
  • The person being accompanied sends the invitation and can end the connection at any time.
  • Companions see the day’s reminders and can see when important things are done.

Two ways, one app: your own everyday life or accompanying another person.

When independence helps both sides

Even 15 minutes make a difference.

500 people × 15 minutes of support per week = 125 hours.

Explaining a letter. Answering a question. Translating something. Reminding someone of an appointment. For one person, it’s often just a few minutes – across many people, it adds up.

digibeg focuses on exactly these small things: helping people understand, organize and handle more of them on their own.

More independence for people. More time for personal support where it is truly needed.

For companions

Enabling digital participation together.

digibeg helps people understand information, overcome language barriers and manage their everyday life more independently.

This helps organizations, relatives and users alike when the same everyday hurdles keep requiring support.

  • Relatives & family

    When family or friends support someone in everyday life and want to know that important things are taken care of.

  • Migration & integration

    When language and complex information make everyday life harder.

  • Care & support

    When people need support with appointments, reminders, documents and organizing everyday life.

  • Social service providers

    When digital participation is part of the daily work of supporting people.

digibeg does not replace care or support. It helps people do more themselves.
